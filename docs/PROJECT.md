# Funding radar

Which Munich-area startups just raised, who is hiring, and where a product designer's chances are best. Live at [funding-radar-two.vercel.app](https://funding-radar-two.vercel.app).

One screen: round date across, chance up, round size as the bubble. Open roles, news and a private application tracker sit on the same page. The data is public (rounds, Ashby, Personio, Google News) and the tracking stays in the browser.

Owner: Denys Sergushkin.

## Done

- Round date × chance chart, search, quick filters, company panel and in-browser tracking.
- Chance ring and factor bars draw in when a company opens. Reduced motion shows the finished score.
- README demo and an Open Graph preview of the chart.
- Filtering or searching the chart shrinks leaving bubbles away and pops returning ones in. The "N on the radar" count under the title rolls. Reduced motion updates both instantly.
